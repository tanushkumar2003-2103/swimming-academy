Important Safety Information for Parents

Children must always be supervised by qualified coaches or designated adults during lessons and anytime they are near water. Swimming lessons build foundational skills and familiarity, but never guarantee drowning prevention, and children should never be considered safe around water simply because they attend lessons. Adult vigilance is always required.

Learning swimming fundamentals in childhood creates lasting aquatic comfort and physical competence. In our supervised environment, children learn to respect water, understand safety boundaries, and develop essential stroke mechanics at a gentle, supportive pace.

Developmental Advantages

Benefits of Swimming for Children

Swimming supports healthy childhood growth, cognitive balance, and emotional resilience in multiple ways.

🏃‍♂️

Physical Activity

Engages all major muscle groups simultaneously, encouraging healthy posture, stamina, and cardiovascular vitality through low-impact movement.

🧠

Coordination Development

Synchronizing arm pulls, flutter kicks, and rhythmic breathing strengthens cross-lateral motor skills and spatial balance.

🌟

Confidence Building

Achieving personal swimming milestones—such as an independent float or first deep-end glide—boosts lasting self-assurance.

💪

Fitness & Endurance

Natural water resistance builds lean functional strength and lung capacity in a gentle, joint-friendly format.

🎯

Focus and Discipline

Listening to coach directions, taking turns in lane lines, and practicing stroke sequences cultivates attentiveness and patience.

🛟

Understanding Water Safety

Instills lifelong habits regarding pool boundaries, respecting deep water, following pool rules, and remaining calm in unexpected situations.

Progression Pathway

Four Progressive Training Levels

Every young swimmer advances through four clear stages at their own readiness level.

01

Water Familiarization

Getting comfortable in shallow water, relaxed breath holding, bubble blowing, and assisted floating without anxiety.

02

Beginner Level

Unassisted front and back floating, torpedo kicks with kickboard, side breathing introduction, and calm treading practice.

03

Intermediate Level

Full freestyle coordination, backstroke propulsion, introduction to breaststroke kick, and continuous 25-meter laps.

04

Advanced Level

Four competitive strokes, flip turns, endurance interval sets, and preparation for club training or water sports.

Parent Information

What Every Parent Needs to Know

Clear guidelines to help your child prepare for comfortable, productive, and safe swimming lessons.

👁️

Class Supervision

Instructors maintain direct visual contact at all times. A parent or guardian is required to remain on the premises during Little Swimmer sessions.

🎒

Training Preparation

Arrive 10 minutes prior to class time to allow your child time to use the restroom, shower briefly, and get accustomed to the pool deck atmosphere.

🩱

Appropriate Swimwear

Fitted swimwear (trunks/one-piece suits), well-fitting silicone swimming caps, and comfortable anti-fog swim goggles are required.

🧼

Hygiene Expectations

A quick pre-swim shower is required to keep water clean. Swimmers with open abrasions or acute illness are requested to rest at home.

📢

Parent Communication

Instructors offer brief progress updates at the end of every monthly milestone block to discuss your child's confidence and stroke gains.
